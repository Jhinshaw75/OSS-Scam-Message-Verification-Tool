Verified Florida & Federal Resource Directory

Connect directly with official state and federal protection agencies:

  • Operation Senior Shield Portal: Learn about statewide fraud alerts and prevention initiatives at the Operation Senior Shield Website.
  • Florida Department of Elder Affairs (DOEA): Access statewide aging services and official resources at the DOEA Official Website.
  • Florida DOEA Elder Helpline: Call 1-800-963-5337 or visit the DOEA Elder Helpline Portal.
  • Florida Attorney General Senior Protection Team: Call 1-866-9NO-SCAM (1-866-966-7226) or visit the AG Senior Protection Directory.
  • Florida Abuse Hotline (Exploitation & Neglect): Call 1-800-962-2873 or report online via the DCF Abuse Reporting Portal.
  • Federal Trade Commission Reporting: Submit online fraud complaints directly at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Official Compliance Note: Free resources like the Florida DOEA Elder Helpline and federal reporting portals never charge fees to investigate fraud.
